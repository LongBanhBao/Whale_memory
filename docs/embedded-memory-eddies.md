# Portraits embedded in water eddies

Reuses the existing blue `xrw-vortex.webp` artwork to create substantial
water rims around the portraits, rather than a white halo or thin bright ring.
Two masked water layers surround each inset portrait. The portrait has a dark
underwater backing, no white glow, and feathered edges inside the water opening.

Four staggered positions follow the central spiral and overlap its outer water
without covering the main swimming aperture. The lower-right photo is offset
outward to keep its face clear of the foreground portal lip.

All SVG connector routes and their CSS, the portrait halo, and shimmer elements
are removed. The visual relationship comes from composition, shared water
texture and synchronized current direction. No new bitmap generation is used.
The whale path, timings, three photo groups and scene navigation are unchanged.
