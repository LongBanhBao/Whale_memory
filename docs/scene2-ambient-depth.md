# Scene 2 — ambient depth

The approved whale, vortexes, portrait placement and swim path remain
unchanged. A deterministic, scene-local atmosphere now gives the journey more
depth without competing with those subjects.

Three broad SVG currents cross behind the gates. Each combines a blurred teal
body, a slow pearl glint and a very restrained warm echo, so the lines read as
layers of moving water and quietly inherit the nostalgic warmth of the
portraits. Their fixed Bezier paths keep screenshots stable, while a small
journey-progress offset adds camera parallax.

Eighteen rimmed bubbles are grouped along the outer 29% of the viewport rather
than scattered through the whale's passage. Four of them occupy a near layer
between the whale and the foreground gate, creating depth without covering a
face. Six faint line-art relics — jellyfish, sea fronds and spiral shells —
float beside the ruins. Cyan, pearl and muted gold keep them part of the same
underwater palette instead of reading as separate illustrations.

The far atmosphere sits below the portal plane; the sparse near bubbles sit
above the whale but below the foreground lip. Both layers ignore pointer input,
are hidden from assistive technology and contain no raster asset. Mobile keeps
only ten bubbles and four relics. Reduced-motion mode leaves a sparse static
composition and disables every new animation.
