# XRW water-memory artwork

Reference: `../LK/XRW.png`, preserved unchanged. Four circular memories feed
curved streams into a central vortex. Existing photo groups, whale movement,
scene duration, next button and synchronized near/far portal halves are unchanged.

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool; optimized with Sharp to
`public/assets/scene/xrw-vortex.webp` (1024 × 1024, alpha preserved).
The original PNG remains in Codex's generated_images directory.

Final prompt:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Input image: XRW.png is a style reference only. Asset type: isolated water vortex texture for an animated underwater website. Create ONE centered circular whirlpool, face-on, with thick flowing cobalt and turquoise water ribbons curling clockwise toward a small open dark-blue central eye. Match the reference's detailed illustrated liquid, bright white foam, translucent cyan splashes and tiny water droplets. No people, no portraits, no surrounding four circles, no text. Square composition, entire vortex visible with generous clear margin. Genuinely transparent background outside the circular swirling water, preserve alpha. The center should be translucent/open for a whale to swim through, not a solid white light. Rich layered water volume, not thin neon rings.

The texture rotates inside the fixed portal plane. CSS masks keep the eye open
and create four smaller water rims around the photos. SVG connectors carry
progress-driven foam toward the center, keeping motion synchronized on replay.
No extra portraits from the reference are included.
